## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. No build step, no framework (user choice).

## Users

Primary: qualified massage therapists (Level 3 and above) in and around Brighton & Hove who want to expand into advanced clinical work. They already have a practice, and they are comparing CPD options on credibility, depth and how hands-on and focused the teaching is.

Secondary: couples who want to learn to give and receive a full-body massage together in a private, guided setting.

Language: English. The audience is UK-based.

## Product Purpose

Intention Massage Academy is the teaching arm of Olga Zemskova's clinical practice. It passes on her clinical experience and her approach to "intentional, informed touch". Success means a therapist books a 1:1 CPD course, or a couple books the workshop, via WhatsApp.

## Positioning

The teaching is private and 1:1, and comes from a practising advanced clinical therapist whose daily work involves complex cases (chronic pain, fibromyalgia, oncology, women's health, trauma-related tension). A therapist trains with one clinician who still treats patients every day; they do not sit in a group classroom.

## Product Principles

1. Clinical credibility first. Therapists buy depth, so show what they'll actually learn, not spa atmosphere.
2. One teacher, one student. Every page should make the privacy and focus of 1:1 teaching tangible.
3. Booking is a WhatsApp message away, and the page should never hide that.
4. State only what's true. Prices and course names come from the source, and unknowns stay unstated.
