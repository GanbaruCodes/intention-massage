## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a clinical assessment form. A therapist chooses a course by marking where on the body it lives. It refuses the spa-academy default of soft photo, serif headline and three course cards.

OWN-WORLD: White carbonless form paper printed in a single deep form-green ink (#0e5a43) with ruled fields, box numbers and section letters. The duplicate copies are canary yellow and pink, used as whole regions: canary for the CPD course table, pink for the Couple Workshop copy. Blue ballpoint for handwritten entries. Red ballpoint is reserved for marking courses and for the booking action. The form text is a condensed grotesk, and only the pen entries are handwritten.

STORY: A therapist sees that this is clinical teaching from a working clinician, delivered 1:1. They pick a body region, read what the course covers and costs, and book on WhatsApp. Couples find their workshop on the pink copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Form header strip (Form IMA/1, sections A–D used as navigation). On the left, a huge condensed headline, "Mark where you want to go deeper.", then ruled fields for candidate, treatment plan, fee and days, then a red "Book on WhatsApp" button. On the right, a large front/back body chart taking about 60% of the width, with nine numbered marks: red rings at the head, shoulder, low back, pelvis, knee, lower abdomen (women’s health) and left calf (myofascial, 1 day), plus two whole-body brackets, deep tissue beside the anterior view and myofascial (3 days) beside the posterior view. The canary and pink copy edges show at the right margin.
